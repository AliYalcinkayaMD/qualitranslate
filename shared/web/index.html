    SCIENTIFIC TERMINOLOGY

    Your bilingual lexicon

    Define the terms your team has agreed. The app flags mismatches; it does not silently rewrite speech.

    Required term mismatches receive a major flag; advisory preferences receive a minor flag. Preferred terms and alternatives are checked in context. This lexicon is excluded from blind back-translation.

    Translation context & reporting

    Record what your team actually did. These details help explain translation decisions in your methods.

    Optional team instructions

    Source-specific additions are excluded from blind back-translation.

    How the method works

    Each API stage uses a separate, stateless request. Back-translation receives translated text only. Manual back-translation requires a fresh conversation for every batch. Reconciliation is checked against the original, with human review recorded separately. Separate requests to the same model are not independent human translators. Exact prompts and responses are included in the download.
